Barari ka Bhoot

A haunted haveli at night: kitchen, bathrooms, storerooms, garage, garden and basement. Nine armed monsters roam it. Each vehicle needs its own items: follow the coloured beams, collect a set, then reach that vehicle. Puja Ghar is a safe room: monsters cannot enter and you heal there. Find all 8 diary pages for a secret ending. Shift or Run sprints, G or Throw lures monsters. Weapons: you start with a bat and a pistol, and shotguns are hidden in the house. Keys 1, 2, 3 or the Weapon button switch; click, F or Fire attacks. Granny, Grandpas and the lizards can be killed. Slendrina, the Nun and Tung Tung Sahur can only be stunned. Green crosses heal and batteries recharge your torch. Press E or tap the button to open doors, hide in wardrobes, or use grey keys on red locked doors.

Phone: left thumb walks, right thumb looks.
Computer: WASD and mouse.
